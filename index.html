Thanks for applying to join MedPal. Our verification team will review your application within 1–2 business days. You'll receive an email at dr.kennedy@medpal.com when there's an update.

What happens next
  1. We verify your license, NPI, and uploaded documents.
  2. You'll get a call or email if anything looks unclear.
  3. Once approved, you'll get instructions to set up payments via our merchant partner.
  4. After payments are connected, your profile goes live and patients can find you.
