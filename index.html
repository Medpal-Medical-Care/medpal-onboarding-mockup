Provider acknowledgment

By continuing, you confirm that all information submitted is true and accurate to the best of your knowledge. MedPal verifies your license, NPI, and supporting documents before activating your profile.

You agree to maintain a current Certificate of Insurance for the states where you practice and to update your credentials as they change. Misrepresentation may result in suspension or removal from the platform.

MedPal handles your documents in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable healthcare data regulations. You can revoke consent at any time from your provider portal.

Required documents

Before you continue, please make sure you have the following ready to upload.

  • NPI number (10 digits)
  • Active state medical license for each state where you practice
  • Medical school diploma (PDF, JPG, or PNG)
  • Any board certifications you'd like to display
  • Certificate of Insurance attestation
  • Government-issued photo ID (front and back)
  • 10-second verification video
